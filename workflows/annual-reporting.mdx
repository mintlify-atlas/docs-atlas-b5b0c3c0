---
title: "Annual reporting workflow for cluster coordinators in MARLO"
description: "How coordinators upload deliverable evidence, update OICR maturity, and complete AR synthesis sections, and how PMU generates donor-ready reports."
---

The **Annual Report (AR)** phase is where a full year of planning and monitoring activity is formalised into a structured, evidence-backed record. In MARLO, AR is the third phase of the annual cycle (after POWB and UpKeep). All data from planning and progress monitoring carries into AR pre-populated — coordinators focus on providing final evidence: uploaded artefacts, DOIs, dissemination records, and updated narrative fields.

The AR phase produces two parallel outputs: the cluster-level record (owned by coordinators) and the program-wide synthesis (owned by PMU), both of which feed directly into donor reporting.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Cluster coordinator">

    ## Cluster coordinator reporting workflow

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Confirm the AR phase is active">
        Check that the phase chip in the page header shows the current AR year (for example, `AR 2025`). If the chip shows a different phase, use the phase switcher to navigate to AR — but be aware that changes save into whichever phase is active.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Review and update the Project Description">
        Open the **Description** section. Confirm that the cluster narrative, objectives, and partner list still accurately reflect the year as it played out. Update any fields that changed significantly from plan to reality.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Complete deliverable reporting">
        Open the **Deliverables** section. For each deliverable:

        - Update the **status** to reflect its final state (completed, ongoing, cancelled).
        - Upload the deliverable artefact directly, or provide a **DOI** or repository link if the output has been formally published.
        - Record **dissemination evidence**: conferences, webinars, training events, or media coverage where the deliverable was presented.
        - Confirm or update the **Open Access** deposit — whether the output is accessible in CGSpace, a journal with open access, or another approved repository.
        - For deliverables with a DOI, MARLO can retrieve metadata from the CGSpace integration to pre-fill some fields.

        <Warning>
          Deliverables without an artefact upload, DOI, or dissemination record may be flagged as incomplete by QA reviewers. Confirm with your PMU which evidence types satisfy your program's quality criteria.
        </Warning>
      </Step>

      <Step title="Update OICR maturity">
        Open the **OICRs** section. For each Outcome Impact Case Report registered in your cluster:

        - Review the maturity classification and advance it if the outcome has progressed (maturity levels run 1–6, from initial evidence to widespread adoption).
        - Attach evidence supporting the updated maturity claim (policy documents, adoption data, partner endorsements).
        - Update the narrative description to reflect the year's developments.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Update innovations">
        Open the **Innovations** section. Update each innovation's Scaling Readiness score to reflect actual progress during the year. Add or update geographic and partner information as appropriate.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Complete AR synthesis sections">
        The Annual Report includes program-level synthesis sections that require coordinator input. Navigate to the relevant synthesis pages under **Annual Report**:

        - **MELIA** (Monitoring, Evaluation, Learning, and Impact Assessment) — enter or update learning and adaptive management reflections.
        - **Governance** — update institutional governance and oversight notes.
        - **Communications** — document key communications outputs and audience reach.
        - **Cross-cutting issues** — report on gender, youth, capacity development, and other cross-cutting dimensions.

        Each synthesis section has required fields validated by MARLO before submission is accepted.
      </Step>

      <Step title="Review completeness and submit">
        Use the completeness indicator on the cluster summary page to confirm all required fields are filled. When ready, click **Submit**. The cluster status moves to **Awaiting QA** and reviewers are notified.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

  <Tab title="PMU">

    ## PMU annual reporting responsibilities

    ### Monitoring cluster completeness

    PMU has access to a program-wide completeness dashboard (via the **BI** menu) that shows how each cluster is progressing through AR sections. During the AR window, this dashboard refreshes frequently so PMU can identify clusters that need a nudge before the deadline.

    Use the completeness view to:

    - See which clusters have submitted versus which are still in draft.
    - Identify clusters with a high number of empty required fields.
    - Track QA validation progress across all clusters.

    ### Completing program-level synthesis sections

    Some AR synthesis sections (Financial Plan, Management Governance, cross-program MELIA) are owned by PMU rather than individual clusters. PMU staff access these through the **POWB** and **Annual Report** menus and complete them following the same save-and-validate pattern as cluster coordinators.

    <Note>
      Program-level synthesis sections go through the same QA process as cluster sections. Ensure PMU-owned sections are submitted before the QA deadline.
    </Note>

    ### Generating donor-ready reports

    Once clusters have submitted and QA validation is complete, PMU can generate structured outputs from MARLO:

    - **Summaries / exports** — access the **Summaries** menu to generate PDF and Excel exports of cluster data, indicator contributions, and deliverable lists.
    - **AI Reports Generator** — the AI-assisted reports service (accessible from the **AI** menu) drafts cluster-specific narrative sections grounded in MARLO data. PMU reviews, edits, and approves these drafts before including them in the donor report.
    - **Power BI dashboards** — the BI hub provides visual outputs (deliverables by status, open access compliance, OICR maturity distribution) that can be embedded or exported into donor presentations.

    <Tip>
      The AI Reports Generator produces drafts that cite specific MARLO records as sources. Review citations carefully before including generated text in formal donor submissions — the drafts are a starting point, not a final output.
    </Tip>

    ### Closing the AR phase

    Once all clusters are validated and program synthesis is complete, the Super Admin or Program Admin closes the AR phase in MARLO. Closing the phase locks it against further edits (phase immutability), preserving the final record for audit and historical comparison.
