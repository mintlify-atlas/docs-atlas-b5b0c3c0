MARLO (Managing Agricultural Research for Learning and Outcomes) is a web-based research management platform built for CGIAR research programs. It replaces fragmented spreadsheets and email chains with a single, phase-aware system of record covering annual planning, progress monitoring, deliverables, innovations, outcome impact case reports, and quality assurance — all in one place.

Originally deployed for CCAFS (2017+) and now actively powering AICCRA across multiple African countries, MARLO has supported 11 clusters, 1,440+ knowledge products, 95%+ open-access compliance, and 92%+ quality ratings.

## Why MARLO

Large research programs like AICCRA and CCAFS span many countries, partner institutions, clusters, and donors. Without a shared platform, teams default to siloed spreadsheets and inconsistent reporting. MARLO solves this by providing:

- **A single system of record** — all planning, progress, and reporting data lives in one phase-aware database
- **Embedded quality assurance** — reviewers provide structured feedback inline, not in separate tools
- **Audit-safe history** — past phases are immutable; data only replicates forward
- **Multi-program flexibility** — run multiple CRPs, Platforms, or Centers on one instance with separate roles and configurations
- **Analytics-ready exports** — operational data flows automatically into Power BI via Microsoft Fabric

## Platform lifecycle

MARLO organizes work around annual **phases**. Each phase represents a distinct moment in the research cycle:

| Phase | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **POWB** (Plan of Work and Budget) | Annual planning per cluster |
| **UpKeep** | Mid-year progress tracking |
| **Annual Report (AR)** | End-of-year narrative + indicator reporting |

All data entered in one phase automatically replicates forward to future phases — you never start from a blank slate.
